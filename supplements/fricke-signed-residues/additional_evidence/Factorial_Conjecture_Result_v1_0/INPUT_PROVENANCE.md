# Input provenance and limits

Prepared 4 November 2026 for a separate research conversation.
The manuscript is Fricke_Signed_Residues_Journal_v1_4.tex and its matching PDF, from the current project. It is a research manuscript, not an independently refereed theorem source.

Both evidence ZIPs are copied byte-for-byte from supplied Signed_Weighted_Residue_v1_0.zip and Eta_Frobenius_Lemma_Audit_v1_0.zip. Quick-start code/results are copied unchanged from the signed-residue evidence package; they are not newly independent computations. Prior manuscript versions inside the archives are historical, and do not supersede v1.4.

The p<=997 factorial data contain 166 matching records. They were inspected for count/range/status, not regenerated through the full range for this handoff. FRESH_SMOKE_CHECK.txt records the bounded computations actually rerun. The independent_check script is an inherited second implementation, not a claim of independent human auditing.

The current handoff adds an assignment, orientation, copies of selected source PDFs, a quick-start extraction and bounded smoke checks. It supplies no new proof and makes no novelty claim. All primes in the target satisfy p>=5. No conjectural formula should be promoted on numerical agreement alone.

Original evidence archives may contain their own historical manifests; the top-level handoff manifest independently hashes the nested archives as supplied.
